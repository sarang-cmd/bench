# Stoichiometry
## 1.1 amount of substance
n = m/M
n = V/V_m for a gas at stp
n = cV for a solution in dm^3
## 1.2 ideal gas
pV = nRT
## 1.3 percentage yield
% yield = actual/theoretical x 100
## 1.4 concentration
c = n/V,  V in dm^3
dilution: c1V1 = c2V2
# Atomic structure
## 2.1 electron configuration
fill order 1s 2s 2p 3s 3p 4s 3d 4p
## 2.2 ionisation energy
energy to remove one mole of electrons
from one mole of gaseous atoms
## 2.3 mass spectra
m/z of the molecular ion gives Mr
# Periodicity
## 3.1 trends
atomic radius falls across a period
first ionisation energy rises
electronegativity rises
## 3.2 group trends
group 1: reactivity rises down
group 7: reactivity falls down
group 2: hydroxide solubility rises down
# Chemical bonding
## 4.1 shapes of molecules
2 bonding: linear, 180 deg
3 bonding: trigonal planar, 120 deg
4 bonding: tetrahedral, 109.5 deg
3 bonding 1 lone pair: pyramidal
2 bonding 2 lone pairs: bent, 104.5 deg
5 bonding: trigonal bipyramidal
6 bonding: octahedral, 90 deg
## 4.2 intermolecular forces
hydrogen bonds: N, O, F with H
## 4.3 polar bonds
dipole moment and symmetry
# Energetics
## 5.1 enthalpy changes
q = mc dT,  dH = -q/n
## 5.2 Hess's law
dH = sum dHf(products) - sum dHf(reactants)
## 5.3 bond enthalpies
dH = sum bonds broken - sum bonds made
## 5.4 entropy and free energy
dG = dH - T dS
spontaneous when dG < 0
dS universe = dS system + dS surroundings
# Kinetics
## 6.1 rate
rate = change in concentration / time
## 6.2 rate equation
rate = k [A]^m [B]^n
## 6.3 Arrhenius
k = A e^(-Ea/RT)
## 6.4 half life
first order: t(1/2) = ln2/k
# Equilibrium
## 7.1 equilibrium constant
Kc = [C]^c [D]^d / ([A]^a [B]^b)
Kp from partial pressures
## 7.2 reaction quotient
compare Q with Kc to find the direction
## 7.3 Le Chatelier
pressure, temperature and concentration
# Acids and bases
## 8.1 pH
pH = -log[H+]
[H+] = 10^-pH
## 8.2 strong acids and bases
pH of a strong base: pOH = -log[OH-]
pH + pOH = 14 at 298 K
## 8.3 weak acids
Ka = [H+][A-]/[HA]
pKa = -log Ka
## 8.4 buffers
pH = pKa + log([A-]/[HA])
## 8.5 titrations
at equivalence n(acid) relates to n(base)
from the balanced equation
